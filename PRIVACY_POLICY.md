# Privacy Policy - Kingshot Composition Calculator

**Last Updated:** December 7, 2025

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kingshot Composition Calculator ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Discord bot services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Screenshots and Images:**
- When you use the `/calculate` command, you submit screenshots of battle reports
- These images contain game data (troop statistics, counts, bonuses)
- Images are processed temporarily using OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology
- **Images are NOT permanently stored** - they are processed in memory and discarded immediately after analysis

**Command Inputs:**
- Troop level selections (T1-T10, TG1-TG5)
- Language preferences (when using `/language` command)

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

**Discord User Information:**
- User ID (Discord-provided unique identifier)
- Only used to associate language preferences with your account during the Bot's session
- We do NOT collect or store: usernames, discriminators, email addresses, avatars, or any other personal information

**Usage Data:**
- Command usage for debugging and error logging purposes
- Error messages to improve Bot functionality
- This data is stored in temporary logs and not permanently associated with users

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly DO NOT collect, store, or process:
- Personal identifying information (names, emails, phone numbers)
- Discord messages outside of direct Bot commands
- Payment information (the Bot is free to use)
- Location data
- Device information
- IP addresses
- Browsing history or activity outside Bot commands

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Purposes

**Image Processing:**
- Screenshots are processed solely to extract battle statistics
- OCR analysis converts images to text data for calculations
- Data is used immediately for battle simulation
- No images are saved or transmitted to external servers

**Battle Calculations:**
- Extracted stats are used to simulate combat scenarios
- Calculations are performed locally/on our server
- Results are sent directly back to you via Discord

**Language Preferences:**
- Stored temporarily in memory to display Bot messages in your chosen language
- Resets when the Bot restarts
- Not linked to any permanent database

### 3.2 Service Improvement

We may use aggregated, anonymized data to:
- Fix bugs and improve Bot accuracy
- Enhance OCR recognition
- Optimize calculation algorithms
- Add new features

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

### 4.1 Temporary Data

**In-Memory Processing:**
- All submitted images are processed in RAM (memory)
- Data is automatically deleted once processing completes
- Typical retention: seconds to minutes, never permanent

**Session Data:**
- Language preferences stored in memory during Bot session
- Cleared when Bot restarts or server reboots
- No permanent database storage

### 4.2 Logs

**Debug Logs:**
- May contain error messages and command execution logs
- Stored temporarily for troubleshooting (typically 7-30 days)
- Automatically rotated and deleted
- Do not contain personal information or image data

### 4.3 No Long-Term Storage

We do NOT maintain:
- Permanent user databases
- Historical battle data archives
- User profiles or accounts
- Image galleries or repositories

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 Third-Party Services

**Discord Platform:**

- Discord's own privacy policy applies to their platform

### 5.2 No Data Sales or Marketing

We do NOT:

### 5.3 Legal Requirements
